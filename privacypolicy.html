Data Governance Structure

Regulatory Enforcements & Personal Privacy Architectures

1. Absolute Consent Architecture

In strict accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act legal requirements, no pixel array transmission, diagnostic processing loop, or history mapping protocol will trigger before explicit confirmation is granted on the onboarding page.

2. Cryptographic Envelope Guardrails

All consumer identity parameters, administrative data vectors, and uploaded high-resolution imagery assets are immediately structured inside hardware-level AES-256 bit encrypted storage nodes. Communications across remote endpoints rely strictly on Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3).

3. Differential Privacy Frameworks

During any subsequent training iterations meant to improve our central Convolutional Neural Network models, data transformation algorithms apply aggressive mathematical noise. This strips all identifying meta-tags, turning images into abstract, untraceable feature weights.
